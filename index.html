01 — THE HUMAN NEED

Different lives.
One simple need: to feel supported.

Safety, emotional wellbeing and connection are deeply personal. Yet asking for help can feel difficult — and technology is not always designed around the moment a person needs it.

♡

Women & girls

Safety concerns in public spaces, social or emotional pressure, and hesitation to seek support because of stigma, privacy worries or fear of judgement.

⌂

Older adults

Living independently can bring loneliness, distance from family, difficulty navigating complex interfaces and the need for simple check-ins or reminders.

✳

The gap

Existing tools often solve only one part of the experience. NIVA explores a simpler bridge between a physical touchpoint, a companion app and trusted people.

02 — THE IDEA

Technology that feels less like technology.

NIVA brings together a small tactile pebble and a companion mobile app. A simple button is intended to start a check-in or request help; the paired phone connects the experience to conversations, reminders and a user-selected trusted circle.

The goal is not to replace people or professional care. It is to make the first step toward connection feel more approachable.

03 — MEET NIVA PEBBLE

A wearable with a purpose beneath the surface.

A smooth, polished gemstone-inspired form is proposed to feel like an everyday accessory — not an obvious alarm. The same core device concept can be styled in different ways.

PERSONAL SAFETY + PRIVATE SUPPORT

A discreet way to ask for help.

In the proposed flow, a deliberate button pattern sends a signal over BLE to the paired phone. The app can then initiate a configured, consent-based alert to selected trusted contacts, with location and timestamp only when enabled.

Demo only: no SOS is sent and no location is shared.
SIMPLE DAILY CONNECTION

Support, without complicated steps.

A single press is intended to start a daily wellbeing check-in. The app can offer optional voice or text conversation, hobby prompts, stories and gentle reminders selected by the user.

Demo only: this interface does not contact family or trigger a reminder.

TRUSTED CONTACTS · USER CONTROL

Your circle. Your choices.

Users are intended to add and manage trusted people. An alert can be configured through the paired phone and a messaging or calling service. The app should show whether an alert was sent or delivery confirmed — never imply that help has arrived.

07 — PRIVACY, SAFETY & RESPONSIBILITY

Your trust is
part of the design.

NIVA is intended to handle personal information with care. Privacy and safety are design requirements — not claims of completed certification or security testing.

Explicit sharing choicesUser-managed trusted circleOptional location permissionPrivate companion conversationsClear alert statusData minimisation & deletion controls
Important boundaries

• AI is intended to support reflection, not diagnose conditions or replace qualified counselling.

• The system should not infer danger from emotion or silence. Users need clear ways to request human help.

• Remote alerts depend on the paired phone, configured services, battery, permissions and network availability.

• An alert being sent does not mean help has arrived.

• Security controls, consent flows and deletion mechanisms must be implemented and independently tested before real-world use.
08 — DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

Start with what matters.
Validate before scaling.

A responsible path from concept to a usable product begins with one dependable flow.

PHASE 01

Understand

Interview intended users, map needs, accessibility requirements and consent expectations.

PHASE 02

Prototype

Build a basic pebble using a controller, button, vibration feedback and BLE connection.

PHASE 03

Test

Validate one flow: press the pebble, open a private app check-in and optionally alert a trusted contact with consent.

PHASE 04

Improve

Use usability, reliability, privacy and safety findings to guide future features.
